Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn patterns for a subscription-based business. The objective is to identify key factors that contribute to customer churn and provide actionable insights to improve customer retention.
The analysis combines Python data analysis and Power BI visualization to explore customer behavior, churn patterns, and business opportunities to reduce customer loss.

Business Recommendations

• Encourage customers to switch from month-to-month plans to annual contracts through discounts.

• Improve customer onboarding experience during the first year.

• Offer loyalty rewards or retention offers for high-risk customers.

• Provide better support for customers with high monthly charges.


Key Insights

* Overall churn rate is 26.58%, meaning about one quarter of customers leave the service.
* Customers with Month-to-Month contracts show the highest churn rate compared to yearly contracts.
* Customers using Electronic Check payment method churn more frequently.
* Most churn occurs among customers with short tenure (new customers).
* Customers with higher monthly charges tend to churn more.
